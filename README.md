# 台灣小吃卡包 TCG Pocket (Taiwan Food TCG Pocket)

[台灣小吃卡包 TCG Pocket](https://jasminechuang.github.io/taiwanfood_mystery/) 是一款互動式的台灣小吃卡片收集網頁應用程式，讓使用者透過模擬撕開卡包的趣味體驗，收集全台 40 道經典美味與在地小吃圖鑑！

---

## 📖 專案介紹 (Traditional Chinese)

* **核心玩法**：每日登入即可免費領取卡包，享受緊張刺激的「撕卡包」開箱體驗！
* **收集目標**：解鎖並收集總計 40 道台灣經典好滋味與在地小吃圖鑑（目前圖鑑已解鎖 37 / 40）。
* **主要特色**：
  * **台灣小吃極品包 (VOL. 01)**：收錄全台經典巡禮的精選卡片組合，每包內含 5 張小吃卡片。
  * **每日獎勵機制**：提供每日免費領取卡包功能（每日免費 +2 卡包），增加收集的趣味與持續性。
  * **完整圖鑑系統**：隨時檢視 40 道全圖鑑的收集進度與解鎖狀態。

---

## 📖 Introduction (English)

[Taiwan Food TCG Pocket](https://jasminechuang.github.io/taiwanfood_mystery/) is an interactive web-based card-collecting application celebrating iconic Taiwanese street foods. Users can enjoy the thrilling experience of opening card packs to collect a total of 40 classic Taiwanese culinary delights!

* **Core Gameplay**: Log in daily to receive free card packs and experience the fun of tearing open packs.
* **Collection Goal**: Unlock and complete the collection of 40 classic Taiwanese food cards (currently at 37 / 40 unlocked).
* **Key Features**:
  * **Taiwan Food Premium Pack (VOL. 01)**: Features a curated collection celebrating Taiwanese culinary traditions, with 5 cards per pack.
  * **Daily Reward System**: Claim free card packs every day (+2 free packs daily) to boost collection progress.
  * **Comprehensive Index**: Easily track and view the unlocking progress of all 40 food items.
